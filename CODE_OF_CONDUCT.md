# Code of Conduct

Cinephile is a friendly project. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests and discussions, is expected to:

- Be respectful and patient, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Keep discussion on the project; no harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Not publish other people's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately using the contact method listed on the repository's profile or its security advisory page (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).
